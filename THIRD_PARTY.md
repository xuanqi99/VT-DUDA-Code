## Reuse and redistribution requirements

- **MIT**: retain the upstream copyright and permission notice in all copies or substantial portions. The license texts here are reproduced verbatim from the stated repositories, including unusual upstream attribution wording.
- **Apache-2.0**: include the full license, retain pertinent attribution and license notices, clearly identify changed files, and preserve an upstream NOTICE **if one is applicable to copied material**. Do not replace the Apache terms with the repository's MIT license.
- **Models / datasets / dependencies**: `requirements.txt` lists separately installed packages; it does not grant rights to their code. This repository does **not** include SDXL pretrained weights or third-party datasets. Downloaded [SDXL base 1.0 model weights have their own terms](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md); software license notices above do not license the model weights, derivative weights, or datasets.
- **Missing authorization**: citing a paper or making a repository public is not permission to redistribute an otherwise unlicensed third-party work. When origin or permission is not established, seek permission, remove the code, or replace it with an independently implemented component.
