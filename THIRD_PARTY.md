# Third-party code, provenance, and licensing review

This file identifies material **incorporated into** VT-DUDA-Code, not merely installed at runtime. It is a provisional provenance review, not proof of ownership or permission and not a substitute for checking the exact source revision of every copied segment.

## Scope of the repository MIT license

The root [MIT LICENSE](LICENSE) is intended **only for original VT-DUDA contributions that the named licensor actually has authority to license**. It **does not relicense** independently copyrighted portions of the repository or override their licenses. Because a published paper or GitHub account does not establish copyright ownership, any contributors' or institution's rights in original work **must be confirmed before this licensing change is merged**. Mixed-origin files remain subject to applicable original notices and terms.

## Reuse and redistribution requirements

- **MIT**: retain the upstream copyright and permission notice in all copies or substantial portions. The license texts here are reproduced verbatim from the stated repositories, including unusual upstream attribution wording.
- **Apache-2.0**: include the full license, retain pertinent attribution and license notices, clearly identify changed files, and preserve an upstream NOTICE **if one is applicable to copied material**. Do not replace the Apache terms with the repository's MIT license.
- **Models / datasets / dependencies**: `requirements.txt` lists separately installed packages; it does not grant rights to their code. This repository does **not** include SDXL pretrained weights or third-party datasets. Downloaded [SDXL base 1.0 model weights have their own terms](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md); software license notices above do not license the model weights, derivative weights, or datasets.
- **Missing authorization**: citing a paper or making a repository public is not permission to redistribute an otherwise unlicensed third-party work. When origin or permission is not established, seek permission, remove the code, or replace it with an independently implemented component.

Confirm that the person named in the proposed root LICENSE has authority to grant MIT rights for the relevant original work and that other contributors' original contributions are covered.
3. **Further lineage:** determine the original source/revision for any additional diffusion encoder customizations and remaining adapted ELS/SDAT/MCC utilities; check whether notices were removed from local files during copying and restore them where needed. If any component was obtained from a source with different terms (including model-related repositories), assess that source separately.
4. **Final legal review:** before marking this audit complete or merging, verify all applicable copyright holders, licenses, file-level notices, and redistribution conditions. Absence of a header in a local file is **not** evidence of original authorship or public-domain status.

This review documents available evidence and unresolved rights. It does **not** assert ownership, transfer of rights, or an ELS-specific authorization.
